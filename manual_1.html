Podkicker Manual

SUBSCRIBING

Manually

You need to know which link is used to subscribe to a particular podcast. This is most often not the same as the link to its webpage. Either click on it from your webbrowser (a dialog should appear), or copypaste it into the subscribe-section of podkicker.

Importing from Google Listen/Reader

Go to subscribe->Import->Google Reader. Choose your account in the dialog and click on the feeds you want to import. Note that podkicker currently does not support textfeeds. Dont forget to refresh your feeds when you are done importing.

Importing OPML-file / other podcatcher

If you have an exported OPML-file, save it somewhere on your device and go to subscribe->Import->OPML.

BACKUP/EXPORT

Currently, the only way to keep your subscriptions if you plan to reinstall the application is to save them to an OPML-file. Go to menu->settings->backup, the feeds are saved to a file called podkicker_backup.opml in the rootdirectory of the external storage. This is also how you export your subscriptions to another application like this.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Why cant I delete podcasts or downloads?

Yes you can, every item has a contextmenu that can be opened with a longtap (put your finger on and hold it for a sec)

How do I play downloaded files in my external player?

From the contextmenu in Downloads, choose "Export Downloads". This will move the downloaded files out of podkicker to the public podcast directory of the external storage. Typically this is '/sdcard/Podcasts/'. The files will no longer be handled by podkicker after this.
